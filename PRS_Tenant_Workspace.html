PRS Complaint Workspace

Draft it here at your own pace · paste it into the portal when it is ready
The Property Redress Scheme portal will not let you save a part-finished complaint, so a long submission has to be written in one sitting or lost. This page is the same set of questions with the same character limits, kept on your own computer. Write it over days if you need to, save as you go, then paste the finished text into the real portal in one go. Nothing you type here is stored or sent anywhere. It is not connected to the Property Redress Scheme and submits nothing — the real complaint is still made on their site.

1 · Eligibility

The scheme will normally only look at a complaint once the agent's own procedure has been exhausted.

2 · You and the property

Every letting agent in England must belong to a redress scheme. If they are not in this one they should be in The Property Ombudsman — and if they are in neither, that is itself something the council can act on.

3 · What the complaint is about

Tick everything that applies.

4 · The case itself
