Increasing Principal Sum method · simple interest, s.69 County Courts Act 1984
Worked the way a county court reads it: each overpayment joins the running balance on the day it was paid, and simple interest at 8% a year (County Courts Act 1984, s.69) is charged on that balance for the days until the next payment. How the interest is worked out. Simple interest at 8% a year under section 69 of the County Courts Act 1984, claimed from the date each sum was paid. It is worked on an increasing principal sum: when each payment is made it joins the running balance, and interest for the days until the next payment is charged on that running balance only (balance × rate × days ÷ 365). Interest is never charged on interest. The same total results from charging every payment its own interest from its own date and adding them up. Whether to award interest, and at what rate, is for the court.
